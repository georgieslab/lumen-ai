Capabilities

Everything in one conversation

No menus to learn. Just talk, type, or drop a file — Lumen picks the right tool.

Natural voice chat

Speak freely and hear Lumen answer in 19 neural Amazon Polly voices, across multiple languages.

Vision & PDF analysis

Drop a photo or document. Lumen downsizes it in your browser and reasons over it with Amazon Nova.

Live web research

Grounded answers from the open web, with SSRF protection so private networks stay private.

Open any web page

Type an address and the page opens right inside Lumen. Your click is the approval.

Share a browser tab

A read-only Chrome/Edge extension lets Lumen read the page you choose — and nothing else.

Writes web pages

Ask for a glowing button or a full page. It appears in a sandboxed window over the orb.

Live weather tile

A glanceable sphere-style tile for any city, powered by Open-Meteo.

PDF reports

Turn research and conversations into clean, shareable PDF files in one request.

Make it yours

Switch tone, response style, voice, theme and atmosphere without leaving the conversation.

The interface

A calm, spatial workspace

A liquid-glass design inspired by spatial computing. The sphere reacts to listening, thinking and speaking so you always know what Lumen is doing.

  • Installable as an app on Android and iOS (PWA)
  • Transcript, copy-answer and share built in
  • Responsive from phone to ultrawide
Trust by design

You stay in control

Lumen can read what you share. It can never click, type or submit in your browser on its own. Anything that opens needs your explicit approval.

  • Tab sharing is explicit and read-only
  • Page text is fenced against prompt injection
  • Generated pages run in a locked-down sandbox
